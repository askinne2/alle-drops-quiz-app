(`tests/quiz-bundle-freshness.test.ts`, Phase 4.1 block) — anchored on the

**Method correction worth recording.** The first extraction attempt was written inline in a
`node -e` shell string and reported `EXTRACTION FAILED` against a bundle that was in fact correct.
Cause: shell escaping mangled the regex, and the ad-hoc pattern also omitted the assignment prefix
and backreference that make the `.flat()` anchor unique. It was rewritten to a file and reused the
committed guard's exact regex, at which point it passed. **A failed measurement is not evidence of
a failed artifact** — the same class of error as this project's `grep -c` and `apntly`/`appointly`
traps, hit a third time, here by the orchestrator.

## Off-checklist observations

The plan explicitly asks for anything the checklist did not anticipate.

1. **Button label changes on the last quiz part.** Parts 1–6 render `Next →`. The medical-history
   part (`Step 9 of 10`) renders `Continue`. This appears deliberate — it precedes consent rather
   than another quiz part — but it is a noun change in the same family as UAT defect #6 and is
   recorded here for an explicit keep/change decision. **Not treated as a defect.**

2. **Layout shift on the medical-history part can swallow a click.** Answering "Do you have a
   Primary Care Physician (PCP)?" reveals an info block (*"We recommend that you establish with a
   primary care physician before beginning SLIT."*) which pushes the Continue button down. A click
   issued at the button's pre-reveal coordinates lands on empty space. A human clicking after the
   reveal settles is unaffected; this was an artifact of scripted clicking. **Not a defect**, but
   worth knowing before any future automated pass.

3. **Error copy carries no PHI.** The submit failure below rendered `Could not save assessment` with
   no name, email, or identifier. That is `CLAUDE.md`'s error-response rule holding under a real
   failure rather than only in review.

## Issue encountered: submit 500, environmental, not the reorder

The first consent submission failed with `Could not save assessment`.

**Root cause: `cloud-sql-proxy` was not running.** Port 5436 had no listener, and `.env`'s
`DATABASE_URL` targets `127.0.0.1:5436` per `docs/local-dev-database.md`.

**Proven unrelated to this phase before being dismissed.** A direct
`POST /api/quiz/submit` carrying a minimal payload with **zero Part 7 fields** — no
`testing_status`, no `testing_files`, no year/location/allergens — returned the same
`HTTP 500 {"error":"Could not save assessment"}`. A failure that reproduces without any of the
phase's own fields cannot be caused by the phase's own change.

After Andrew started the proxy in its own terminal, the same probe returned `HTTP 200` with a
`submission_id`, and the browser submission completed and rendered results. Check 8 was then
observed in full.
