<!-- What the browser must be executing, and how the counter is derived. -->

From `app/lib/quiz/schema.ts` `:261-284` — `quizFlowProgress(step, totalParts)`:
`total = totalParts + 3` where the three non-part steps are state_gate, patient_info, consent.
With `QUIZ_PARTS.length === 7`, every label reads `Step N of 10`:
  state_gate -> Step 1 of 10 · patient_info -> Step 2 of 10 ·
  quiz part index i -> Step (3 + i) of 10 · consent -> Step 10 of 10
Post-reorder that makes: **Part 7 (allergy testing) = Step 3 of 10**, Part 1 (nasal symptoms) =
Step 4 of 10, Part 6 (medical history) = Step 9 of 10, consent = Step 10 of 10.

Part 7's content, unchanged by this phase (D-07 — internal item order untouched):
- `testing_status`, a two-option radio: "I need allergy testing" / "I've already had allergy testing"
- subtitle, shown BEFORE the choice: "If you choose 'I've already had allergy testing,' you'll be
  asked to upload a copy of your results (PDF or photo) to continue."
- on `had_testing`, four children reveal simultaneously: `testing_year`, `testing_location`,
  `testing_allergens`, and the required `testing_files` multi-file dropzone plus its
  "This is required to continue" info block

The served bundle route is `/quiz-bundle-js` (the 300s-max-age variant referenced by
`quiz-embed.tsx`). The committed artifact is `public/quiz-bundle.js`.
</interfaces>
