<objective>
Delete the `"medical_history"` `FlowStep` and everything that existed to serve it (D-12), delete the
7+ "Proceed Without Testing" chain and its warning modal (D-11), and delete the now-dead `extra`
payload parameter — leaving one flow in which 100% of patients pass through medical history as
`QUIZ_PARTS[5]` before the outcome page (D-13).

Purpose: HIST-05. After plan 03-01, medical history is a real member of `QUIZ_PARTS`, which means it
now renders TWICE for a 7+ patient — once as Part 6 in the normal flow and again in the bolt-on
`medical_history` step. This plan removes the bolt-on. It also closes the client half of T-3-01: the
only remaining caller that populates `buildPayload`'s `extra` parameter with
`{ personal_history, family_history }` is `handleConsentSubmit`.

Output: a `QuizContainer.tsx` with one fewer `FlowStep`, three fewer handlers, one fewer state
variable, one fewer render branch, and no `extra` parameter; a `ResultsDisplay.tsx` with three
callback props instead of four; and a new source-text guard proving it.
</objective>
