<objective>
Extract `QuizContainer.tsx`'s inline `buildPayload` into a pure `app/lib/quiz/payload.ts`, and pin
D-10 at the unit level.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 5 — "the score and submitted payload are identical whether an
intake was completed in one sitting or resumed" — is the criterion that can silently fail. It cannot
be tested at all while payload construction is a `useCallback` closed over component state. This
plan makes it testable and locks the exclusion list to exactly three fields so nobody can widen it
later to make a failing parity test pass.

Output: `payload.ts` + `payload.test.ts`. This plan does NOT modify `QuizContainer.tsx` — plan
04.2-04 rewires the call site.
</objective>
