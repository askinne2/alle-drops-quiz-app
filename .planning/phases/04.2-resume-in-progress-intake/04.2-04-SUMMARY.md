## Accomplishments

- `QuizContainer.tsx`'s `buildPayload` is now a thin `useCallback` wrapper over `buildSubmitPayload` (`app/lib/quiz/payload.ts`) — one construction site for the submit payload, so the one-sitting and resumed paths cannot diverge (D-10).
- `"resume_offer"` added to the `FlowStep` union with zero edits to the `progressInfo` ternary or `app/lib/quiz/schema.ts`'s `NON_PART_STEPS` arithmetic — verified directly via `git diff` showing zero changes to `schema.ts` and zero diff inside the ternary block.
- A lazy mount-time draft read (`readDraft()` from `draft-store.ts`) is the single branch point for both D-01 (storage unavailable) and "no draft" — `isStorageAvailable()` appears zero times in `QuizContainer.tsx`.
- `handleResume()` restores `patientState`/`patientInfo`/`symptomProfileId`/`answers`, then computes the landing step via the D-09 rule: the lowest `QUIZ_PARTS` index where `isPartComplete` is false, regardless of `draft.step`. Only when every part is independently complete does it honour `draft.step`, recomputing score/bracket through the exact chain `goToConsent` uses. `consentChecked` and `startTime` are never restored.
- `ResumeOffer` renders inside `quizContainer__questions` (not an early return); `RestorationNotice` renders once as the first child on the restored step and is dismissed via `setShowRestorationNotice(false)` in all four navigation handlers that leave the restored screen.
- `tests/quiz-resume-restore-dom.test.ts` — 10 tests through the real `QuizContainer`, seeding `localStorage` directly: D-01 absent state (both "no draft" and "storage throws" render identically), D-05 active-cleanup expiry, D-06 zero-identity offer, RESUME-01 restore with pre-filled answers, D-09 upload-wall survival (including a hand-planted `testing_files` tampering variant), D-08 start-over-from-offer, and consent-integrity (unchecked box, disabled Submit).
- RED proof performed and recorded (see Deviations/Issues below): the D-09 landing rule was temporarily disabled in-session, both D-09 tests observably failed with the expected messages, then the mutation was reverted (`git diff` confirmed byte-identical to the prior commit) and both tests passed again.
